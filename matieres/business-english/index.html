1. Workplace communication

Communication is the exchange of information, ideas or feelings between people. In a workplace, it helps coordinate tasks, execute plans, make decisions, solve problems, improve productivity and maintain relationships and company image.

Misunderstanding gap

Message: "Please finish this ASAP".

Problem: one employee understands "today" and another understands "tomorrow". State a precise date and time.

2. Five components of communication

Source / sender

Creates and sends the message.

Message

The content being communicated.

Channel

The method: email, call or spoken discussion.

Receiver

Interprets the message.

Feedback

Response that helps check understanding.

3. Common communication barriers

Environmental

Noise, distractions and distance.

Language

Jargon or unfamiliar vocabulary.

Psychological

Stress, inattention or assumptions.

Context / tone

Irony or an unclear situation.

Intermediary

Details changed or omitted during transmission.

4. Verbal and non-verbal communication

Verbal

Words, whether spoken or written.

Non-verbal

Gestures, facial expression, posture and eye contact.

5. Effects and solutions

Consequences

Conflicts, rework, delays, wasted resources and lost trust.

Good practices

Use clear terms, adapt to the audience, listen actively, ask questions and request feedback.

Exam takeaway

Sending a message does not prove that it was understood. Check the receiver’s interpretation, particularly for tasks and deadlines.
